Cancel an active timer owned by the current agent. Cancellation is idempotent for an already-cancelled timer; completed timers cannot be cancelled. Dependent unadmitted waits are cancelled atomically with terminal reason `timer_cancelled`, including their fallback deadline. This does not fire the timer or resume the agent. A WorkItem blocked by that wait stays explicitly blocked until a new wait or operator action; independent blockers and already-admitted execution are preserved.
